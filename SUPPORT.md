# Support

forward-zabbix is an experimental proof of concept maintained on a best-effort basis. It is not a Forward
Networks product, has no SLA, and may change without notice.

- Questions and bugs: open a GitHub issue. Do not post credentials, customer names, device names, IP
  addresses or snapshot contents; use synthetic examples.
- Security issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- What was tested and what was not: [docs/validation.md](docs/validation.md).
